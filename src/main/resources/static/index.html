Passenger Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you fly with Aerowing.

When does online check-in open?

Online check-in opens 48 hours prior to scheduled departure and closes 90 minutes before international flights. You can manage your seat selection directly via your passenger dashboard.

What is the baggage policy across cabin classes?

Economy tickets include one 7kg carry-on and one 23kg checked bag. Business Class allows 2 x 32kg checked bags, while First Class grants 3 x 32kg checked bags plus complimentary golf/sports gear handling.

Can I change or refund my booking?

Flight changes are permitted up to 24 hours before departure through your passenger dashboard. Refund policies apply depending on whether standard or flexible fare was selected during checkout.
